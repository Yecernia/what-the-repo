# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) · **English**

[Product overview](README.en.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

I'm [Yecernia](https://github.com/Yecernia), the author of what-the-repo. I want this to be a place where you can ask questions, share your work and learn comfortably, whether this is your first open-source project or you have years of development experience.

## Working together

- Respect each other. Ask when something is unclear, and give beginners room to explain and learn.
- Discuss the problem, code or idea, offer specific suggestions, and accept reasonable disagreement.
- Respect others' time and contributions, and credit your sources.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private messages or material you do not have permission to share.

I do not accept harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, persistent spam or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting a concern to me

If you encounter behavior that breaks these expectations, email me using the address listed on [my GitHub profile](https://github.com/Yecernia). Please start the subject with **[what-the-repo Community]**.

In your email, tell me what happened, where and when, and what outcome you are seeking. Do not publish people's personal information, screenshots of private messages or other sensitive details in public issues or discussions.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## How I will respond

I will review the circumstances, hear from those involved when needed, and share report details only with people needed to address the concern. Depending on severity and repetition, I may ask for a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Serious threats or privacy violations may require immediate action.

If you believe a decision was mistaken, you can explain why and request reconsideration through the same private channel.

These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community spaces. They apply to my own behavior as well.
