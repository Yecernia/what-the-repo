# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) · **English**

[Product overview](README.en.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

what-the-repo welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of learning. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community spaces, including maintainers' participation.

## How we work together

- Be considerate and assume good intent when asking for clarification. Explain unfamiliar terms and make room for beginners.
- Discuss ideas, code and observable behavior. Give specific, constructive feedback and respect reasonable disagreement.
- Credit others' work, respect their time, and accept that a proposed change may need revision or may not be accepted.
- Protect privacy. Share only information needed for the discussion and that you have permission to disclose.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, personal attacks, publishing someone else's private information, spam and retaliation against a person who raises a concern are not acceptable.

## Raising a concern

Contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/Yecernia) through a private contact method they have explicitly published for this purpose. If none is available, open a minimal [issue](https://github.com/Yecernia/what-the-repo/issues) titled **“Private community contact requested”** and ask how to continue privately. Do not include allegations, names of affected people, private messages or other sensitive evidence in that public request. The request itself is public; do not use it if even acknowledging the concern would expose someone.

Once a private channel is agreed, describe what happened, where and when it happened, and the outcome you are seeking. Include only relevant evidence that can be shared safely. Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

Maintainers will consider the available context, hear from the people involved where appropriate, and limit disclosure of reports to what is needed to address the concern. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality where platform or legal requirements apply.

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may request a change in behavior, issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Immediate restrictions may be necessary for threats, privacy violations or ongoing harassment. Where safe and practical, the affected person will receive a brief explanation and may request reconsideration through the same agreed private channel. A maintainer involved in a complaint should defer review to another uninvolved maintainer when one is available.

This is a project-specific community policy. It does not replace GitHub's own rules or the project's software license.
