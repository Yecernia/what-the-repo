The National Internet Security Management Service Platform provides this badge
for display immediately before an approved website filing number in the footer.

No open-source license is asserted for this official emblem. It is not covered
by the project's MIT license and must not be used as project branding or to
imply endorsement. Display is conditional on a configured filing record.
